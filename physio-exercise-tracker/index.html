The Retune app

A physio exercise tracker that helps the routine stick

Put in the exercises your physio gave you, tick them off each day, and see your week fill in. Retune is built by a physiotherapist to help your home program become a habit, not another thing to feel behind on.

What Retune does

  • Your plan, your words. Type in the exercises from your physio's sheet: sets, reps or holds, and which days. Retune never changes the amounts you were given.
  • One tick a day. Today shows one thing: your session. Tap when you're done. If you tap by mistake, undo it.
  • A short version that counts. Busy, tired or sore? Retune suggests an easier version, such as one set instead of three, and you can change it. Doing the short version still counts as done. If you're not sure what yours should be, ask your physio.
  • Tie it to your day. Pick an "After I…" moment, like "after I pour my morning coffee", and add a reminder or a calendar event if you like. The reminder only says "Time to Retune", so nothing about your health shows on your lock screen.
  • See it add up. Your week fills in as you go, and a short habit check every so often shows whether the routine is getting more automatic. Missing a day doesn't wipe anything.
  • A summary for your physio. Make a one-page PDF of what you've done, how sessions felt and any pain scores you chose to record. You decide whether to send it.

Why track at all?

Keeping a record of what you actually do is one of the better-studied habit tools. Across many studies, people who regularly checked their progress towards a goal were more likely to reach it, and the effect was larger when they wrote it down.1 In physio research, home exercise programs delivered through an app or web system led to slightly better self-reported adherence than paper handouts or the physio's usual method, although how much that matters for recovery isn't clear yet.2,3

A tracker won't do the exercises for you. What it can do is make the routine easier to remember, quicker to record, and easier to talk about at your next review. For more on the research, see how to track your physio exercises at home.

How it's different

  • Nothing resets. There's no count to protect and no score that drops back to zero when life gets in the way. Retune shows what you've done, not what you've missed.
  • Built by a physio, for after the appointments. It's for the part that usually goes quiet: the weeks after discharge, or between reviews.
  • Private by default. Your plan and logs are stored in Sydney. No advertising, no tracking tools, and you can delete your account in the app. See the Privacy Policy.
  • No clinic code needed. You don't need your physio to use any software. Bring the summary to your next appointment if you want to.

What Retune isn't

Retune is a general wellness app for building an exercise habit. It doesn't diagnose, monitor or treat any condition, it doesn't give medical advice, and it never changes the exercises or amounts your physio gave you. If a pain score you record is high, it suggests checking with your physio, and that's all it does with it. If you're unsure whether an exercise is right for you, ask your physio or doctor.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes, while it's in testing. We'll tell you clearly before anything changes.

Android or iPhone?

The pilot starts on Android. iPhone comes later. Join the list either way and we'll let you know.

Does my physio need to sign up?

No. You put in your own plan. If you want your physio to see how it's going, send them the PDF summary or show it at your appointment.

Can I use it without a physio?

Yes. Retune also works for any regular exercise or healthy habit you want to keep up. If you have pain or a health condition, check with a health professional before starting new exercises.
