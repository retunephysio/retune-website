Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-01. Applies to the Retune app and retunephysio.com.au.

1. Who we are

Retune is run by Retune Physio (ABN 75 662 599 148), a sole trader business run by a physiotherapist in New South Wales, Australia. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Retune Physio. Contact us at hello@retunephysio.com.au.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. Some of what you put into Retune is health information, which the law treats as sensitive. We handle it with extra care.

3. How we collect it

You give us everything above by typing it into the app, or into the pilot form on our website. We don't get information about you from anyone else.

If you don't give us your email, birth year and agreement, you can't use Retune. Everything else is up to you: you can leave out notes, pain scores and goals.

4. How we use it

We use your information to run Retune for you: show your plan, remind you, record what you do, show your progress, and make a summary you can choose to share. We also use it to keep Retune secure, fix problems and answer your emails. If you email us, we keep the email so we can reply.

We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and never use it for research. We won't send you marketing emails unless you choose to get them, and you can stop them at any time.

During the pilot, and only if you tick the optional box, we look at combined, de-identified habit check answers and session counts to see whether Retune is helping people build routines. Only Retune Physio sees them, we never share them with anyone else, and we don't publish them. You can untick the box in Settings at any time.

5. Rules the app applies automatically

Retune uses simple, fixed rules, not artificial intelligence. These ones only make suggestions, which you or your physio can ignore:

  • Pain notes. A pain score of 6 or more, or 3 or more above your usual, shows a note suggesting you check with your physio. The same rule marks those scores in your physio summary. It is a prompt to get advice, not an assessment.
  • Short version. Retune fills in an easier version for busy or sore days, such as one set instead of three, as a starting point you can change. If you're not sure what it should be, ask your physio. Retune never changes the exercises or amounts your physio gave you.
  • Progress lines. Messages about your week are chosen from your logged sessions.
  • Reminders. Reminders are timed from the days and times you set.

Two rules decide on their own: whether you can create an account (your birth year must be at least 18 years ago), and pausing an account for a short time if it sends too many requests at once. The rules use your birth year, your plan, your logs and your pain scores, which are health information. No person checks each result before you see it.

7. Information sent overseas

Some information goes overseas: your email address and sign-up codes to Resend (United States); your phone's operating system, a random install number and the internet address of the request to Expo (United States) when the app checks for updates; technical logs, including the internet address and phone or browser type used to sign in, to Supabase's logging service in Europe; and, if you use the pilot sign-up form, what you enter there to Netlify (United States). Your exercises, logs, pain scores, goals and backups are stored in Sydney. Your email address at Resend does show that you have a Retune account. Supabase is a US company; its support staff could access data from overseas in rare cases, under its data processing terms.

8. How we keep it safe

  • Your data is encrypted when it travels and when it's stored.
  • Each person's data is locked so only they can reach it, and we test this.
  • The copy on your phone is encrypted, and logging out removes it.
  • Reminders only say "Time to Retune" or "Still time to Retune today", and calendar events only "Retune".
  • We use two-step sign-in on the accounts that run Retune, and only the owner of Retune Physio can reach the data.

No system is completely secure. If something goes wrong, see section 12.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your account and data: until you delete them, or until 24 months after your last sign-in. We'll email you 30 days before we delete an unused account, and signing in keeps it.
  • After you delete: removed straight away from our live database. Backups are kept in Sydney and deleted within 7 days.
  • Code emails at Resend: 30 days.
  • Technical records: deleted with your account.
  • A record that you agreed to these terms: kept for 7 years after you delete your account, so we can answer a complaint. It holds only the version you agreed to, the date and time, and a scrambled one-way copy of your email address that can't be turned back into your email. Nothing about your health.
  • Pilot sign-up form details: deleted from Netlify within 30 days of arriving. We keep our own copy until 6 months after the pilot ends, or less if you ask us to delete it.

10. See, fix, download or delete your information

In Settings you can see your information, change it, download a copy, and delete your account and data (apart from the small agreement record in section 9). You can also email hello@retunephysio.com.au and we'll reply within 30 days. We won't charge you. If we can't do what you ask, we'll tell you why in writing, and you can ask us to note that you disagree.

11. Adults only

Retune is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your birth year at sign-up and don't knowingly keep information about anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

12. If there's a data breach

If your information is involved in a breach that's likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires. We assess a suspected breach within 30 days and tell you as soon as we can.

13. Complaints

Email hello@retunephysio.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

14. Physio and clinic links (not yet active)

Retune may later let you link your account to your physio's clinic. That isn't available yet. Before it is, we'll update this policy, explain what the clinic would keep, and ask you again.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll show you what changed in the app and ask you to agree before you keep using Retune. The version and date are at the top.
