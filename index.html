The gap Retune fills

The hard part starts when the appointments stop.

In the clinic you have a plan, someone checking in, and a reason to keep going. Once you're discharged, the exercise sheet goes on the fridge and life gets busy. Retune is built for that stretch.

People who've always exercised usually keep going. It's part of who they are, and it slots into their week without much thought. If it isn't part of yours yet, every session is a decision, and decisions run out. That's not a willpower problem. The habit just doesn't exist yet.

In the clinicYour physio sets the plan and checks your progress.
After dischargeNo more appointments. The routine is up to you, and if exercise isn't already a habit, it's easy to let it slide.
With RetuneA small daily routine, a short version for busy or sore days, and a summary ready for your next review.
How it works

Four steps, about a minute to set up.

Add your exercises

Type in what your physio gave you, as written. Add your own goals too, like a walk or the gym.

Fit them into your day

Pick your days and a time, or tie them to something you already do, like your morning coffee.

Tick them off

One tap when you're done. On busy or sore days, the short version still counts.

See your weeks add up

Track how consistent you've been, with optional pain scores, and share a summary with your physio.

Built on how habits form

Four ways Retune helps it stick.

Make it obvious

Tie it to something you already do

"After I pour my morning coffee…"

Linking a routine to an everyday cue is one of the most reliable ways to build a habit. Add a time, a reminder or a calendar event too.

Make it attractive

Start with what it's for

"Long walks with the dog."

Write what you want to get back to, in your own words. Retune keeps it in view, so each session connects to something you care about.

Make it easy

A short version still counts

Tired, sore or short on time? Do one set of each and it counts. Keeping the routine alive matters more than a perfect session.

Make it satisfying

Feel it add up

One tap when you're done, a quick "how did that feel?", and a week that fills in. Missing a day doesn't wipe anything. Retune shows what you've done, not what you've missed.

Small proof, not big promises

You don't have to believe it yet.

Confidence comes from proof, not pep talks. Retune quietly collects that proof: the sessions you've done, the weeks you did more than you planned, how far you've come since you started. Over time, the evidence does the convincing.

And once the habit is automatic, Retune is designed to step back, with fewer reminders and a weekly check-in instead of a daily one. Most apps want you to open them more. We'd rather you didn't need to.

For physiotherapists

Keep patients connected to the plan you gave them.

Retune doesn't replace you. Your naturally active patients often don't need much help. Retune is for the ones who do: it helps them keep doing what you prescribed, and brings them back to you with something useful when it's time for a review.

  • Patients enter your program as written. Changing a dose asks them to confirm they're going against your plan.
  • Patients can choose to share a one-page summary of what they logged: sessions done, the weekly pattern, pain scores and current exercises.
  • Pain that's higher than usual prompts the patient to check in with their physio.

Retune is a habit tool for your patients to use themselves. It isn't a clinical monitoring system. Interested in recommending it to a few patients? Get in touch below.
